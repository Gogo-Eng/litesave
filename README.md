# LieSave Savings Group App

A **LiteSave** blockchain-inspired savings group for students.  
Members contribute weekly based on their tier, and **earn 20% return on the total pooled amount**.

---

## Features

- **Student Registration**  
  Enter name and select savings tier  
- **Live Dashboard**  
  Shows total pool, weekly interest, and member count  
- **Tier-Based Contribution**  
  Tier 1: ₦10,000 (5% personal interest)  
  Tier 2: ₦20,000 (10% personal interest)  
  Tier 3: ₦30,000 (20% personal interest)  
- **Withdraw & Remove Members**  
  Simulate withdrawal and remove students from the group  
- **Real-Time Updates**  
  All calculations update instantly  
- **Clean, Responsive UI**  
  Works on phone, tablet, and desktop  
- **No Installation Needed**  
  Just open `index.html`

---

## How It Works (Math)

```text
Total Pool = Σ All Member Contributions
Weekly Interest = Total Pool × 20%
Your Interest = (Your Contribution / Total Pool) × Weekly Interest
